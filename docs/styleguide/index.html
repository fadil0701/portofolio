04 · Tokens

Elevation

Elevation is a three-part system: surface step + border + shadow. Shadow alone is insufficient in dark mode, so a shadow token must always be paired with a surface change.

Shadow noneflat, border only
--shadow-e1raised card, row hover
--shadow-e3dropdown, popover, menu
--shadow-e4modal, dialog, drawer
--shadow-glowone hero element, max
Focus ring — --color-focus-ring 5.45:1 light / 10.99:1 dark · outline only, never shifts layout · press Tab

16 · Signature

Signature Devices

These four devices are what make the design recognisable instead of generic. They are the only permitted decorative layer — anything else is noise.

1 · Signal rule

A 4px brand rule with tick marks, opening a major section. One per section, never more.

2 · Chamfer

The neo-industrial notched corner. Never combined with a large radius on the same corner.

3 · Grid overlay

Technical atmosphere behind hero and diagram sections. 4.5% opacity ceiling, masked with a radial fade, always aria-hidden.

4 · Grain

Fixed film grain across the whole page at 3.5% opacity, applied once on the root wrapper via .os-grain.

Anti-generic checklist
Banned outright Purple-blue AI gradients · glassmorphism everywhere · emoji as icons · floating blob backgrounds · centred hero with a three-word slogan · pill radius on everything · stock illustrations
Required instead Asymmetric editorial splits (7/5, 8/4) · mono micro-labels as engineering signal · oversized serif index numerals · real content over placeholder prose · one accent moment per viewport
